# Spikewave logo (approved 15 Nov 2026)

Chosen by the owner after six rounds (boards kept in `brand/logo-*` folders for reference only).
Generated by `tooling/logo_final.py` (which builds on `logo_p1.py` and `logo_premium.py`).
Never edit the PNGs by hand; change the script and rerun it.

## The logo
- **Name:** "Spikewave" in Source Serif 4 (weight 560, display optical size), the website's heading face.
- **Line:** one hand-drawn line under the name, with the spike under the "k" of "Spike" and a calm wave under "wave" (round 6 "P1b"), drawn slightly thicker than in round 6 (5.8% of cap height).
- **Icon:** a white serif S above the line on deep navy. The line is a calm wave under the S, and the spike sits just after the S.

## Slogan
"Purpose-built solutions for real-world problems" (owner, 15 Nov 2026). Set in Inter, muted grey, under the logo on the banners. It replaces "Mobile apps for Android and iPhone".

## Rules
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the "S".
- Don't recolour the name, stretch it, add shadows, glows or gradients, or put it on busy photos.
- The line always goes with the name in the full logo. Below about 180 px wide, use the icon instead.
- Each app keeps its own icon. This logo is for Spikewave itself (social profiles, website header, documents, end cards).
